About Nass Learning App

Nass Learning App is a student learning project offering topic summaries, exam-style practice and simple educational games.

Learning and exam notice

Practice questions in this app are original learning exercises, not official WAEC, NECO, JAMB or BECE papers. Always check your school syllabus and official exam-board instructions.

Privacy

This version stores lesson completion and game scores in your browser's local storage. It does not create accounts or send progress to a server. Do not enter private information.
